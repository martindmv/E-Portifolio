# Glossary

## Core Domain

**Portfolio**: The central entity of the application. A digital resume representing an individual's professional identity, comprising personal information, skills, experiences, and projects.

**Skill**: A specific competence, tool, or proficiency associated with a Portfolio (e.g., "Python", "Project Management").

**Experience**: A professional or academic background entry linked to a Portfolio, detailing roles, companies, and durations.

**Project**: A concrete achievement or work piece linked to a Portfolio, often including a description and a URL.

## Roles

**User**: An authenticated individual who owns and manages their own Portfolio.

**Admin**: A privileged user with the authority to manage all Portfolios within the system (e.g., deleting entries).

## Engineering Terms

**Landing Page**: The initial web page (route `/`) serving as the public entry point and marketing showcase for the E-Portfolio project.

**Tracer Bullet**: A minimal, functional implementation used to validate the architectural design and deployment pipeline (routing, templaries, static files) before full-scale development.

**Spec**: The technical and functional requirements document (e.g., `docs/spec.md`) defining the scope of a feature.
